On 2026-10-29 the B-Dry header and after-FAQ paste files were finished and verified after the first build, but the
plugin-side rule changes ran 6–7 review rounds per job and Justin asked "why is this taking way too long… are we
done?". Told the reviews were finding real edge cases in other designers' files, he said "that's great. Keep it
going" and "it's not a bad thing, that's good."

**Why:** he values the thoroughness; what bothered him was waiting without seeing that the part he needed was usable.

**How to apply:** as soon as a deliverable he can use (a paste file in Downloads) is verified, tell him it's ready and
offer the paste steps, even while plugin work keeps looping. In long review loops, say every few rounds what each round
found and roughly how long is left. Don't cut review rounds to save time unless he asks. See [[run-without-stopping]].
