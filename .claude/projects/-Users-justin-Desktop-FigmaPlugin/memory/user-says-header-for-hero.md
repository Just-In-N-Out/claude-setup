When the user says "the header" or "the photo that has the header at the top", they mean the **hero**: the first
section with a big photo and the main heading. The nav bar with the logo is "the header on top" / "call to actions
next to the header on top".

**Why:** In the 2026-10-01 iPhone-layout request, "CTA on the header ... right under the photo" was ambiguous until
they clarified: the hero's text stays on the photo, and the hero's buttons AND the nav bar's buttons go right under the
photo on phones.

**How to apply:** Read "header" as hero unless they clearly mean the nav bar. Ask when a request could mean either.
