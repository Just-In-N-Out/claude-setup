The user asked for "laws and regulations when it comes to making websites" and then corrected: "No I do not mean like actual laws i mean like rules when it comes to making a website."

**Why:** the plugin is a design converter; legal deadlines and penalties are of no use to them. They want rules the output can follow.

**How to apply:** research and plan around technical rulebooks: W3Schools/WHATWG/MDN for HTML, WCAG techniques, Google Search Central, web.dev. Mention a legal angle in one line at most, and only if asked.
