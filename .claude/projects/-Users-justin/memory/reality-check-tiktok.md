Started 2026-11-26. Justin asked for a TikTok page "influencing young men" with black pill content. Claude declined to promote the ideology; Justin picked the **reality-check angle** in the question that followed (the videos push back on "it's over"). Format he picked: vertical short, 45-60 s. Personal project, not Leorna: no Leorna logo or end card. The page has no name yet.

**Style Justin asked for after seeing v1 (2026-11-26), in his words:** "more flashy", "more engaging", voice "a bit more enthusiastic because someone's watching this on TikTok", "remove the episode 1 at the top and all that", "more animations, like people", "try to write a story", and "it should be like a story that you're telling it, like, bro, I don't want facts and shit". v1 was a dark fact-check with on-screen sources and stats, and that is what he rejected.
**How to apply:** tell a story about a character in a friend's voice ("Bro! Let me tell you about a guy"), with animated people, bright scenes, hard cuts, shakes and big word-by-word captions; no stats, citations, source lines, episode labels or progress bars on screen. The message still runs against the black pill (my reading of the agreed angle, which he did not withdraw). Voice: Kokoro `am_fenrir` (measured widest pitch range of the local male voices, with `am_puck`); `am_michael` was the flat one.

On 2026-11-26 he also asked for gospel videos ("a bit to do with Christianity, like spreading the gospel", "pick a story from the gospel", under a minute, holding attention throughout); he did not say whether they go on the same page. No depiction of Jesus, Bible reference at the end (my choices). **His reaction to the bright cartoon gospel v1 (2026-11-26):** "this is too catchy too bright make it a bit like idk modern i guess add music. Change the narrator voice to someone that [fits/hits]". **How to apply:** gospel videos get the dark minimal look, not the Marcus look: near-black frame, faceless figures, one gold accent, serif italic key words, slow fades, calm past-tense narration with no "Bro!", and a music bed. **Voice and music (2026-11-26):** he said "ok great" to the dark look but rejected the deep male voice (blend `am_michael,am_onyx`) and the ambient no-beat music: "i do not like the voice and the music make it a bit more energetic or like a women high pitch tone". v3 uses the female voice `af_heart` at 1.08 and a 112 BPM synthesised score with a beat (`music.py`); his reaction to v3 is not known yet. Whether the Marcus-style videos should also change is not known.

Files in `~/Movies/RealityCheck/`: `ep02-lost-son-v3.mp4` (current) and `ep02-lost-son-v2.mp4`, both built from `ep02-lost-son-v2/` (dark minimal gospel template; its `script.md` lists what differs; ffmpeg `alimiter` needs `level=false` or it boosts back to 0 dB; `music.py` synthesises an original score with numpy from the timeline cues and `mix.sh` ducks it under the voice; film grain needs crf 23 or the file is 168 MB); `ep02-lost-son-v1.mp4` with source in `ep02-lost-son/` (the bright version he rejected; bright-style template: `P()` there draws robed characters with beard, sash and rags options, plus `mouths()`, `walk()`, `hop()`); `ep01-marcus-story-v2.mp4` with its source in `ep01-story/`; `ep01-80-20-rule-v1.mp4` with `ep01/` is the rejected fact version (its `script.md` still holds checked sources).

**Build method:** `tts.py` makes the voice plus `timeline.json` with a time for every word (Kokoro's `result.tokens` carry `start_ts`/`end_ts`); `video.html` builds each scene from that file, so scenes, characters (`P()` draws an SVG person with movable arms, legs, head and mouth) and captions follow the words and a new voice or speed needs no retiming; `render.js` seeks paused Web Animations to each frame time, screenshots at 30 fps and pipes JPEGs into ffmpeg. One animation per element and property: put a second move on a wrapper, and use `fill: 'none'` for shakes and flashes. Toolchain gotchas: [[demo-video-toolchain-mac]].
