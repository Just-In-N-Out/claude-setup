# figma-to-bricks: long pitfalls (lines moved whole from SKILL.md Pitfalls; load when one matches)

## Bricks output and hand-built CSS

- A bar painted inside an unpainted section frame becomes section → band → Container (`section.ts` `bandIR`): a rule marking "the section's Container" must look through bands, and a Figma wrapper's own max width wins over a widening rule after it. Give such rules a wrapped fixture variant; real headers are drawn this way (header-bar Task 4b: fixtures green, hydraulics unchanged). A child swapped into a laid-out fixture row before `snapshot()` keeps x/y 0 and becomes the row's first layer: give it the old child's x/y. A fixture for a design wider than 1440 needs every section frame at the design width (else the 1440-wide site-parts sections fold into one page section); assert the pattern is recognised before asserting an effect is absent, or the absence check passes vacuously. A logo frame filled by a same-size picture is no drawn logo (the picture becomes the frame's cover), so such a bar is never a header. A fixture page of only two sections must be drawn at least 1.5× its width tall (`page-frame.ts` `pageLike`), or `bandsOf` makes the whole page one section and section-level guards can't be reached. Normalise merges a frame wrapping one vector into a single vector named after the frame and keeps only the frame's own `vector.svg`: put an icon fixture's drawing on the outer node. Debug files carry an SVG only for vector layers and frames made of vectors (`extract/dump.ts` `vectorOnly`): a ☰ built from rectangles, or a picture of one, has no drawing to read, and a fixture vector without `vector.svg` stands in for a v1-file icon. A fixture row of builder `vector()` icons is merged into one drawing by `vectorOnly`, losing the icons' links: test linked icons with small `image()` layers. Header-guard fixtures: `bar-parts.ts` `action()` takes a frame holding one text as a menu item before its linked-group check (a linked-group fixture needs 2+ words), and `ctaLike` needs a pill at least 32px tall (29px chips never reach a "no call to action" guard). In a free-drawn fixture frame, a rectangle spanning the whole frame becomes the frame's own fill after normalise (a "background layer stays a layer" negative needs an absolute child of an auto-layout frame). `designs.ts` `space()` turns the gap between two self-painted sections into a margin in the page colour: code that makes sections out of a painted frame's children must give those gaps the frame's paint, and assert the seams in Chrome (after-faq-sections AC2); every "is it painted" reader (`rhythm.ts` `paints()`/`edgeOf`) must also see paint a section got that way (`sectionIR` `behind`), and a gap that is really paint (a frame's padding a kept part shows) must be held inside the painting section before `rhythmOf` runs: `space()` drops free gaps at every boundary the rhythm sets, so fixing only the read still loses it. Overlap-based overlay assignment (`bandsOf`-style, default `bands[0]`) breaks for auto-layout flow children, which never overlap: probe any split-a-frame rule with a narrow flow child above, between and below the parts. A rule that decides with `siteFooter` and reshapes the list before `leaveOut` calls it again can disagree with it (the thin-bar path looks only directly above a bar ≤ `BAR_MAX`): keep the split only when the final call finds exactly the tail, and probe a 1px rule and a 120px+ wordless part between the columns and the © bar. `site-header.ts` `hasMenu` counts any row of 3+ short one-line items or 3+ links, so a © bar with three legal links and a call-to-action band with three pills both read as "a menu": a rule asking which part holds a footer's links must probe both, plus a part holding a section heading, a row of three cards each linking elsewhere (also "a menu"), and both sides of any exclusion that saves a page part: "has a big heading" also matches titled link columns and a menu row with a large typed logo. Apply such an exclusion in every branch that asks the same question (after-faq-sections reviews 3–4). Also probe the footer's own brand block drawn several ways (a row or a centred column of logo, line and "Contact us" pill; a typed logo; a logo stacked over its line; a square logo at the 120px photo threshold), contact or info cards, painted icon circles with image glyphs and client logos, with and without a page part above: none reads as a menu, yet all are the footer's, and a "page part" sign like "holds a Button or a painted box" matches them all. A careful split needs an allow-list of page-part shapes, not a deny-list (after-faq-sections review 5). A probe meant to reach that split needs titles of three or more words: a one- or two-word title ("Our process") reads as a logo typed in words, so the frame leaves whole as on base and the probe silently tests only the fallback; keep a separate two-word probe that expects base (after-faq-sections verifier 2: 16 of 26 probes). The allow-list's refusals inherit their recognizers' blind spots: probe each with the drawings it misses (`openingHours()` needs 2+ schedule lines in one text; `contactsIn()` reads only visible words, not a "Email us" linked to mailto:; a word count that takes "&" as a word and "Co."/"Inc." as a sentence end; `boxed()` reads a box's own paint while `painted()` also counts a covering rectangle). A geometry check over a part's IR ("a line under words") reads only flowing layers (`placement === 'flow'`): a layer placed over the part's edge (a badge) and a covering rectangle are shapes too. Phone numbers and emails in words are already `tel:`/`mailto:` run links (`src.text.runs[].link`) when understand runs (only whole-layer links reach `IRNode.link`), so a guard on run links must leave contact links alone. Links to other contact schemes (sms:, callto:, skype:) are dropped by normalise and reach understand as plain words, so a contact-link guard never sees them. A Figma link on a section frame (a header bar drawn as its own section) reaches neither the section IR's `link` nor its Container's (both null); only `src.clicks` shows it, so a "the whole bar is linked" guard reads `src.clicks` along the path, not `linksHere` alone. A small layer lying across two free-drawn parts groups them into one section of `band` children: a rule reading parts must refuse when anything but bands flows there (probe a badge across a heading and a band).
- A phone-only copy made at understand with `show: "phone"` is shown by `phone.swap` at 767 even when the adapt rule meant to style it never runs (its plan found no tier): don't make the copy, or hide it at every tier, when that condition can be false; every copy-making feature needs a fixture where it is false (copy shown nowhere, payload = switch-off paste: emit leaves out a copy shown at no tier but keeps its keys in `assignIds`). A copy pushed into a row is also read by row readers that take `n.children` directly (`adapt/kinds.ts` `iconRow` counted the menu's Nav as an item): exclude it in every reader and prove with a switch-on/off witness of every element outside the copy at every tier above its fold, on the emitted paste too (split base keys from `:<tier>` keys, cut the `@media (max-width: …)` blocks out of `_cssCustom`, compare with whitespace removed: a leftover newline reads as a desktop change) (phone-menu review R1-1 and fixer, 2026-11-02).
- Bricks prints a linked Image (no tag) as its link: `<a id="brxe-…" class="brxe-image … tag" href><img></a>` (id and classes on the link, custom attributes on the img, `a.brxe-image{display:inline-block}`; Bricks `image.php`, live DJ staging, darksalmon element CSS); `test/browser/render.ts` follows it since 2026-11-03 (it used to print `<a href><img id>`, which made a correct logo rule look 60px too small). Convert's default (pictures to WordPress) makes Images; test helpers' `inlinePictures: true` makes Divs, so a browser receipt measuring a linkable picture runs both picture modes, with stand-ins of each picture's own shape (a linked Image stacked in a column takes its file's ratio) (phone-menu auditor + fixer 2).
- Hand-built paste CSS: Bricks' Image with tag `figure` is inline-block (inside a link it gains a line box and the link's width cap: give it `display:block; max-width:none`); `object-fit: cover` can't crop a logo's transparent margins (clip the figure, scale the img to its ink); the repo's test renderer prints element custom CSS after all its settings CSS, but live Bricks 2.3.9 prints the desktop control rule, then the custom CSS, then the tier rules (≤991, ≤767): give a custom media query both a min- and a max-width (e.g. 992–1024, a range Bricks' tiers miss) so it means the same in both, with no !important needed (hero-logo-size planner, staging post-155.min.css offsets). Live global-class CSS prints last (the inline `bricks-frontend-inline-inline-css`, after `post-<id>.min.css` and `style-manager.min.css`) as `.cls.brxe-<element>`; the renderer prints classes first as bare `.cls`, so at equal specificity a class beats an element's custom rule live but not in the renderer (web-compare planner). Bricks' own element CSS (`.brxe-section/container/block` flex column and ≤767 wrap, `.bricks-button` inline-flex) sits in `@layer bricks` live, so any unlayered rule beats it whatever its specificity; the renderer prints it unlayered, so write restores explicitly and never rely on `revert-layer` (web-tiers planner, frontend-light-layer.min.css). Element controls print on `#brxe-<id>`, so a class's `:hover`/`:focus-visible` can't override them: put every property the class changes in the class (`.brxe-button.<class>`). An element `_border` with width and style but no colour makes Bricks 2.3.9 write `border: <w>px <style> var(--bricks-border-color)` (#dddedf, light grey) on the id, beating any class border colour; give `_border.color` or keep the whole border in the class. The repo's test renderer writes no colour there, so tests miss it (quote-button-hover planner).
- **A visible rectangle or seam around a picture:** inspect the emitted shadow before assuming alpha loss or a blend-mode bug. Validate any replacement against both transparent cut-outs and opaque rounded photographs; a fix can remove the square yet regress ordinary shadows. Do not copy a box-shadow blur radius directly into `filter: drop-shadow`: the filter uses Gaussian standard deviation, so the corresponding box-shadow blur radius maps to half that value. Do not chain multiple drop shadows as independent Figma shadows: later filters shadow earlier results and can invent additional silhouettes. See `references/rendering-validation.md` for the reproduction and decision procedure; multiple-shadow support or fallback needs an explicit scoped decision.
- **A feature needs a Bricks setting no sample shows:** search `reference/bricks` and `src`, read a live page's printed config and saved CSS (a Slider (Nestable)'s `data-splide` shows what Bricks uses for unset keys; `uploads/bricks/css/post-<id>.min.css?f2b=<random>` often proves an earlier paste's "unverified" key already survived paste and save, and prints Bricks' own element base CSS, e.g. slider-nested's flipped previous arrow and centred slides; `themes/bricks/assets/js/bricks.min.js` shows an element's behaviour, e.g. a Toggle opens its `data-selector` or the first `.brxe-offcanvas`, but never its paste keys), then consult the official schema/docs before making Justin supply a sample. Archive authoritative evidence with its schema version and mark a documented example as documented—not copied from Bricks. If that evidence is absent or insufficient, ask for one concrete action: add a Button, set the option, right-click → Copy, paste its JSON. Never guess keys. If the documented schema is newer than the installed Bricks version, distinguish source-backed implementation from installed-version compatibility and retain an explicit paste/copy-back check.

## Fixtures and text

- Generic text fixtures use the builder option `family`, not `fontFamily`; `fixedText`/`segmented` default to 24px line-height. For per-layer diagnostics, assert final warning counts with distinct source IDs sharing identical names, targets and reasons, covering success and fallback; differing names hide message-based deduplication. Repeat diagnostic probes on page layers and rendered card/variant subtrees, including generated copies. Give a large-font ticker its actual single-line height/line-height before diagnosing marquee recognition. `text()` overwrites a passed `layoutSizingHorizontal` and the row builder never stretches a FILL child: set `{ ...text(…, { width: <row's rest>, inkWidth }), layoutSizingHorizontal: 'FILL' }` after. `normalise/infer.ts` reads a row's widest gap (≥ 40px, ≥ 2× the others, no picture) as an auto push, so a two-item row with one wide gap is never tight: use three items with equal gaps. An arrow icon beside a box's words makes the box the plugin's own page link (`page-links.ts`); a box ≤ 120px tall holding only short one-line texts or icons is a `labelBox` (`normalise/label-box.ts`; no paint or direction test, so an unpainted label stacked over its value counts too): code meaning "a button" also needs painted/outlined or `sideBySide`, the paint possibly on a box holding only the label box at any depth (a painted "Call now / number" frame around an unpainted column): carry the boxes above through the single-child recursion and read their paint only once a label box is reached, since `rowEven`'s stand-in boxes have no fills (djtrees-top-align); an auto-layout column taller than its free-drawn section is read as placed. When a Chrome spill check fails on a new fixture, render it with the new marks stripped first: if it fails as drawn too, the fixture's widths are the cause. Probe a new rule with the designs that fool it: a go-word rule (`page-words.ts` GO) against nav menus and footer link columns (one item changes, its siblings don't); a rule on fixed or drawn widths with a free-drawn words + arrow group inside a row (without auto layout it infers a fixed-width row with no free room). A rule that leaves out items by counting alike siblings must also check the holder is that kind of group (a menu: every flowing item one line of words or wordless), or a mixed section column passes. Narrowing a rule to leave out a kind has its own blast radius: probe the common layout sharing that structure (a blog card's category pill beside meta words of its font and size looks exactly like tabs). `IRNode.parent` is the SceneNode a layer sits in, not its IR parent: reason about IR siblings through `walkIR`'s second argument or a carried ancestor path. A `copyIR` root comes back with `parent: null`, `placement: "flow"`, `parentFlow: null` and `sizing.w: "fill"`: geometry that needs its holder comes from the IR parent in the walk. To prove a new `payloadProblems` guard passes on main's output, put only the new `payload.ts` in a `git archive <main>` tree and run the unit suite and replays there (every fixture conversion calls `assertPayload`). Fixtures and corpus passing doesn't prove a new fatal guard can't fire on users' designs: grep every producer of what it refuses (for links: every link set after nodeIR, e.g. `phone.ts` band copies, `list-rows.ts`/`list-links.ts` PDF links) and probe each with a generic fixture. Moving an element's link to another IR node drops it from `emit/page-addresses.ts` (matched against `n.src.link`/`pageLink`): carry where it came from. A wrapper whose IR `flow` is null reads as zero room in `adapt/least.ts` (`least`, `reading`, `content`): judge it by the inner box holding the layout. A fixture testing "inside a box that clips" needs a clipping frame with a corner radius, a spill or a placed layer: `style/paint.ts` writes `overflow: hidden` only then. Fixture `frame()` defaults to `clipsContent: false` while Figma's Frame tool clips by default: probe any feature that carries a layer past its frame's edge (wide-screen bleeds, edge extensions, hanging cards) with `clipsContent: true` plus a placed layer too (31.2's grouped edge photo passed unclipped and was cut at 1920 clipped; djtrees-panel-edge planner). Probe rounded corners too: a `clip-path: inset()` replacing a frame's `overflow: hidden` drops the cut at its rounded corners, so carry the radii with `round` (each corner grown by any outward extension, so shadows stay whole); with a drop shadow one clip can't both keep the shadow in a corner's notch and cut the children there (djtrees-panel-edge review and fixer). Fixture `snapshot()` never visits `targets`: call `snapshot(targetFrames)` first, or overlay/Navigate target frames keep relative boxes. Layers moved or replaced after `snapshot()` keep a stale `absoluteBoundingBox`: call `snapshot(roots, extra)` again before converting. Fixtures put the page frame at canvas (0, 0), real frames never sit there: a feature reading absolute geometry needs a probe with the root moved (e.g. x 3000, y −700). The builders' `text()` sets `absoluteRenderBounds` to the whole box unless `inkWidth` is given, so a rule reading Figma's render width needs fixtures with a realistic `inkWidth`. An adapt rule overriding CSS that emit builds outside `Style.parts` (a mark's CSS, Motion) holds only if emit writes that base CSS before the tier `@media` blocks: check the order first. A fixture adding an Open link or Navigate to an instance appends to its reactions (`[...(c.reactions ?? []), …]`): replacing them drops the main's While hovering. Recognition predicates read after `understand()` see the hover layers `planHovers` adds last, so compare decided outcomes (phone copies, split marks, showcase marks) instead. To prove a plan reader treats a wrapper like the unmarked layer, compare `planLayout(page, styleAll(page, scene), pageFacts(page))` least, width and row at every tier, and `content` only from tablets down (it throws at `fluid` on body text). A feature reading line geometry from a text box also needs a one-line text in a taller box: `styled()` takes `boxHeight` and `valign` (headline-marker; it shifts the SVG baselines as Figma draws them), while a FILL-height text still needs `layoutSizingVertical: 'FILL'` and `layoutGrow` set by hand. A multi-slide (Splide) fixture needs slides of different content lengths: Splide stretches every slide to the tallest, so a painted child that doesn't grow leaves a blank strip. An icon placed from a library is a frame around a vector with no paint of its own (and a circle button's paint is its backdrop): read its colour from its SVG drawing and its size from the drawing's bounds. Splide's `interval` runs from the start of each change (speed included), while Swiper's delay and Figma's After delay start after it ends. `normalise/infer.ts` `group()` runs `inferLayout` with default options on the CUT groups v2 makes, so a per-frame option passed down `inferLayouts`' walk never reaches them: gate such options on `n.figmaType !== CUT`. Normalise can't predict understand's section roles exactly (a frame spanning a free-drawn section is a band through `fullPart`, painted or not). A feature keyed on layers placed in a box must also be probed with them grouped (Cmd+G) or in a plain frame: the wrapper becomes the IR box, so shares and shapes land on it and phones drift while the design width stays exact. Count calls to action with `markCtas`'s whole predicate (`ctaLike`, no short tag in a chip row, `pillMiddle`): `ctaLike` alone takes a painted form field (words at its left) for a pill. Calls to action have two consumers, `markCtas` and `heroOf` → `heroButtons` (hides button-like layers in a hero on phones and copies them into a band): exclude a feature's own pills from both, and probe it as a hero's beside-media (a free-frame root with 2–3 sections placed at y, the picture an image layer). Figma's SVG export of a stroked vector puts `fill="none"` on the root `<svg>`, not the path: reusing the body under a new root must carry it over, or the path fills black; check for unwanted dark pixels, not only the stroke's colour. `instance()` shallow-copies its main, so instances share text objects: clone an instance's text before changing its words. Never `assert.deepEqual` IR nodes or SceneNodes (they hold whole scene trees): on a failure Node's diff can run the test out of memory (SIGKILL, no message); compare keys or identity. Figma's text SVG in the debug file (`text.svg`) gives every styled run's exact line and place, but groups its tspans into `<text>` elements by fill, not reading order: sort tspans by baseline y, then x (trailing spaces and `&#10;` sit inside them). `SceneNode.box` is relative to its own frame: in a two-selected-frame fixture, put the second frame's layers lower than the first frame's end, or an overlap check skips the pair before a same-frame guard can be mutation-tested. A frame without auto layout is always fixed-height (`rawSizing` defaults to fixed), so only a section made of one non-frame layer tells a "no auto layout" guard from a "fixed height" one (closing-cta). Negative controls for a section rule pass for the wrong reason when built loosely: overlapping children in a frame without auto layout are placed (the words stop flowing), a fills-only frame over the whole box folds into its fills, an opaque fill drops the fills under it, and on a 390-wide drawn phone design a short first section reads as a top bar (the next section becomes the hero); build them in auto-layout sections with `layoutPositioning: "ABSOLUTE"` overlays and assert the subject isn't the hero (djtrees-storm-photo: 3 mutants survived until rebuilt). The fixture builder `image(name, opts)` ignores a passed `fills` like `vector()` does (it writes its own `imageFill` after spreading the options): assign a faded, tinted or adjusted image fill after the call, or the fixture tests a full-strength photo (djtrees-faint-picture). Likewise a lone frame against the LEFT of a frame without auto layout infers a column with the page's 64px padding (the right side gets 0), and a frame that is its parent's only child with the parent's own box flows rather than covers: add a layer on the other side, or a sibling (djtrees-panel-edge). A layer drawn straight in a free section's frame over its only flowing column stops inference (`infer.ts` `lift` needs three items in a design frame): place it as an ABSOLUTE child of the column, which is lifted to the section as in real designs; and a ~24px caption in a placed card is tagged h2 and, lifted before the Container in walkIR order, becomes the section's first heading (djtrees-side-art). For an "as base" browser AC that can't run base code, compare head's render with the same payload minus the feature's own declarations (keep key order, drop emptied `_cssCustom`), and prove in a receipt that head's and base's stripped payloads are byte-identical (djtrees-panel-edge). A "keeps today's output" test that only asserts nothing appears is proven byte-identical by re-running it in a scratch head copy with its `convert` import swapped for a stand-in converting a `structuredClone` with base's and head's `src` (two module instances) and recording equality per call; calibrate on a positive AC's test, which must report a difference (djtrees-stack-lines auditor). To name rebase clashes with a sibling job on the same rule files, rehearse the merge in scratch (archive the sibling's head, `patch -p1 -F0` this job's src/test diff, finish rejected hunks by meaning, run both jobs' tests): it finds test-level clashes reading misses (djtrees-stack-gaps review: 3 and 2 failures, 37/37 with both guards). Comparing two `npm run check` runs by test name, number repeated names by their own count (the unit suite has duplicate names), not the run's size (djtrees-stack-lines auditor). Three or more equal-width unpainted items with no text of 8+ words stay two per row on upright phones (`shapes.ts` `small`: each ≤ 25% of the row, no paragraph or photo, (inner − gap)/2 ≥ 140): a fixture meant to stack needs an 8+-word text or one item wider than a quarter of the row; and a payload-diff receipt maps IR nodes to element ids (`assignIds` over `walkIR(page.sections)` keys, emit's order) rather than looking elements up by layer name, since a named layer's element can have no `label` (djtrees-stack-gaps). A section is always at least 60% of the page wide (`page.ts` `bandsOf` makes anything narrower an overlay), so a section-level guard's negative fixture must be that wide, or it only exercises the overlay path (closing-cta review 2). A fixture blast-radius scan (every export, base vs head) calls `builders.ts` `resetIds()` before each fixture and builds every node after it (nodes built before share ids with the page built after: "Paste refused: duplicate id"; in a loop of controls make kids lazy) (they share the id counter, so a new file sorting earlier shifts every later fixture's ids), picks exports by calling them with no arguments rather than `fn.length === 0` (optional parameters), and swallows promise results (closing-cta verifier 2: 5 false changes without it) and unhandled rejections (`process.on("unhandledRejection", …)`: `panel.ts` stand-ins reject later and kill node several files on, silently skipping the rest; djtrees-storm-photo).

## Fonts

- Google supplied-face metadata: validate supported literal family/style syntax before unquoting or `familyKey` normalization (normalization intentionally erases punctuation). Never reject every token merely because it is a reserved family keyword: valid unquoted names such as `Noto Serif` and `Example math` need positive controls; keep standalone/leading generic rejection and malformed-delimiter controls separate. Keep Unicode/opposite-quote family controls and signed/fractional oblique angles in deg/grad/rad/turn, one/two endpoints within ±90deg. Unsupported descriptors stay unconfirmed, while accepted raw CSS and request policy stay untouched; metadata hardening is not approval to rewrite/defer away valid range faces.
- **Google metadata and projection use different eligibility rules.** Reuse the typed declaration scanner for executable metadata instead of a first-match regex: comments can hide the real descriptor or manufacture a fake first match, and repeated supported numeric weights use the last declaration. Keep projection's strict duplicate/escape/raw-text safeguards separate. Trace the metadata reader's `confirm` callback before claiming a reporting-only patch leaves all CSS unchanged: fixing confirmation can unblock already-supported numeric range copies. Identify and explicitly allow updates to tests that pin the old parser limitation; prove the real rebuilt panel's rows, notes, copied payload and request trace, not just Node metadata.

## Process and evidence

- **Separate Figma preservation from disposable test-folder cleanup.** Record the exact resource covered by a no-deletion instruction; do not silently broaden or narrow it. If normal browser/unit/corpus tests remove their newly created temporary folders, identify that specific operation early and ask for a narrow exception while independent implementation continues. Once granted, update the plan, brief and handoff and steer the live owner; do not leave an obsolete gate hold. Permission covers only verified run-created disposable folders and their generated contents—not Figma layers or temporary clones, existing folders, user/project source files, sibling artifacts, worktrees or branch cleanup. Preserve durable evidence outside disposable folders and keep unexecuted gates explicitly unverified. These scoped permissions belong in the task records, not as global user preferences.

- **Audit diagnostic exports before requesting them or calling them read-only.** Check document, temporary-node, store, selection, clipboard and network side effects: Save debug file reads the current selection and can create/remove temporary clones while exporting state pictures. Accept recording/inspection consent once, retain its exact exclusions in the plan and worker brief, and do not re-ask an already-resolved cleanup question. When no deletion includes temporary copies, exclude that exporter without modifying source to bypass the rule. Preserve the open output/runtime/selection and use only verified nonmutating observations. A UI screenshot is not a source snapshot, and a current-source export is not retrospective proof of the last conversion's input; if permitted evidence cannot answer the missing-source question, report that gap rather than claiming the fallback resolves it. Do not treat `pictures:false` as nonexporting: snapshot extraction still invokes text/vector SVG helpers. A separately approved safe metadata reader needs a closed read-only projection/capability, not the broad snapshot exporter.

- **Offer a safe release split instead of letting an expanded rendering feature hold a proven core fix indefinitely.** Identify the structurally supported subset, the exact existing behaviour retained for unsupported cases, its warning and remaining compatibility proof. Ask Justin to approve that split before reversing an earlier all-or-nothing decision; changing models is not compatibility evidence. Once approved, record the replacement release scope and retained deferred scope in the plan and board, reuse committed work without resetting it, and start the bounded builder immediately. Require regression controls for both the corrected subset and unchanged fallback, then a fresh independent review before merge. Keep wider research separate so it cannot silently re-block or expand the approved release; the partial release still owes its own gates and paste check.
- **Trace shared recognizer consumers before freezing the plan's file allowlist.** Search callers and capture their outputs before changing a recognizer, because a negative classification can enable later heuristics rather than merely remove one link. For `contactsIn`, contact linking precedes inferred page linking, so rejecting an ID/date can unmask whole-card eligibility; `card-title.ts` also uses recognition to exclude title candidates. Add integration regressions for new page addresses, title ties and explicit linked pills before the recognizer fix. Prove the fixture permits card inference with a no-caption positive control and a separate section heading: a lone section card or a two-text field-shaped fixture can hide the regression. If changing those consumers needs unapproved policy/files, preserve the proposed patch and RED proof, restore production source, commit passing consumer controls/records and stop for a scoped revision. Report this as test-only groundwork, not an implemented phone fix.

- **Show something only in the Bricks editor, or only live** (reviews-show planner, 2026-11-20): Bricks' own element settings `_hideElementFrontend` (drawn in the builder only; it and its children never print on the live page, their scripts and styles not enqueued) and `_hideElementBuilder` (live page only), since 2.0.1 (Bricks 2.2 `base.php` L2634-2638, L2703; both keys in the 2.4.2 builder). The editor canvas inserts a Code element's server HTML with `innerHTML` and never re-creates scripts, so any loader or script-drawn widget is empty in the editor; to be seen there it must be carried as elements. Element conditions always pass in the builder (`conditions.php` L877). A test or tool that counts, maps or word-checks a paste against the live page reads it through `test/browser/render.ts` `shownIn` (`tools/site-live.ts` skips front-end-hidden elements; reviews-show builder).
- **A scan-dependent class is missing from an importer paste (stay class, a mark-driven rule)?** Check the capture across ranges before blaming Bricks: a page builder's sticky script unsticks on resize, so `web.position: fixed` depends on whether the design-width read caught it; `web.code.nodes[p].scripted` per range and Elementor `data-settings` in `.marks` are the reliable record (header-template planner: home 2026-11-20 scan scripted only in 413-440, no stay class; Basement scripted in 1367-up, stay class).
- **A scan's `media.json` can key a picture by another resized copy than the one its background loaded** (`?fit=1920,725` vs the declarations' `?fit=1024,387`, same file): read a background's natural size from `web.natural` under the address in the declarations and find its Media item through the library mapping, never by matching the two addresses (photo-repeat-2 planner, Crawlspace Repair).
- **A pasted page shows bare in Bricks (elements there, no styles), or styled until a fresh tab:** Bricks keeps every global class in one list and the builder writes the whole list back on save, so (1) a stale second editor tab can wipe classes another added, and (2) a list grown too big may not persist at all: B-Dry's 13 importer pastes held 3,970 classes, 15.4 MB, 11.7 MB of it 13 per-page `<page>__site-fonts-<4>` classes with embedded font files (~896 KB each); a page pasted and saved in one tab ('Superb') was bare again in a fresh tab (2026-11-23, job fonts-class-size). Bricks writes the whole list as one option on every Save and ignores whether WordPress wrote it (2.2 `ajax.php:1924-1952`, `helpers.php:2686`; WP `option.php:964-967`), so a list past MariaDB's default 16 MiB `max_allowed_packet` says saved and keeps nothing; anything a paste puts in a global class counts against that one row, summed over every page of a site. Test persistence with a fresh tab after Save; keep one Bricks tab open.
- **Before planning a fix for a look Justin reports from a screenshot of the old site, check the capture's own picture of the part** (snapshot `renders[i].png`, base64, taken at scan time): a later or logged-in screenshot can differ from what the scan saw (legal-pages: the scan drew the titles bold, as the paste did; his next-morning shot didn't).
- **The code route's part root (`select.ts` `partRoot`) takes one root and drops a part's extras** (`parts.ts` assigns e.g. a theme's `article > header > h1.entry-title` outside the builder's document): find them by a measured root's children whose `web.source.path` lies outside the part path (legal-pages, rule F).
