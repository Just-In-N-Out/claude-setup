But the scan did read the site's drawer half-styled at some desktop widths on both pages (home worse: three width ranges, because the site refused the drawer's stylesheet; About one), and that is a real fault in both pastes.

## Root cause (the scan defect), with evidence

- `src/web/capture.ts:306-364`: a width range's stylesheets are checked once, when its page load is accepted (`openStyled`, `openDesign`), before anything is pressed.
- `src/web/read/opens.ts:71-77` (`open`): after the press it waits only until the box shows; `src/web/read/opens.ts:136` reads the box's code at once (`readCode`). Nothing waits for, or looks at, a stylesheet the page's script adds because of the press, so a late or refused one leaves a silent hole in that range.
- `src/web/read/opens.ts:217-238` (`assembleOpens`) then records the hole as "these declarations are dropped at this range", and the paste writes them as bounded runs (`@media (min-width: 1367px)` and `(max-width: 1079px)` on the About drawer's Row, Box and Link).
- Receipts (evidence folder `~/.hermes/plans/figma-to-bricks/evidence/offcanvas-wave/planner/`): `opens-holes.txt` (home `elementor-popup:27`: 11 declarations dropped in each of 1025-1366, 1024-1024, 992-1023; About: 11 in 1080-1366), `capture-ranges-home.txt`, `capture-ranges-about.txt`, `home-scan-refused-summary.txt` (3 x `dialog.min.css` 403), `draw-pastes-mid.log` and `pastes/*.png` (the drawings), `paste-ids.txt`, `bricks-2.4.2-paste-ids.txt` (Bricks' paste routine).
- Same cause on any site: every box a press opens whose styles load on demand (Elementor pop-ups and drawers on every Elementor site; any lazy-CSS modal).

## The fix (general, scan side only; nothing under `src/core`, `src/plugin`, `src/shared`, `src/ui` changes)

- New `src/web/read/open-sheets.ts` (opens.ts is at 283 lines): `pressSheets(s, before, waitMs)` polls `sheetFacts` (`src/web/styled.ts:35`) until no stylesheet link absent from `before` is on its way (`pendingSheet`) and the list of links is the same on two polls in a row (`POLL_MS`), at most `WAIT_MS`; it returns the links the press added: `got` (applied) and `lost` (`lostSheet` or still on its way at the limit, with styled-ranges' own words: "answered 403", "failed to load", "still loading").
- `src/web/read/opens.ts`: `readOpens` takes the page's links once before the first press, and after `open()` and before `readCode` calls `pressSheets`; the reading carries `sheets` when the press added any (`OpenReading.sheets`).
- `assembleOpens`: a range's reading of a box that lost a stylesheet another range's reading of the same box got is left out, so that range takes the nearest read range's reading (the existing rule) and isn't listed in `read`; one note names the widths and the stylesheet ("capture the page again to read them"). A stylesheet lost at every range is the site as the capture got it: the readings stay, one note. `assembleOpens` returns its notes; `src/web/capture.ts:496-498` adds them and passes `opts.styleWaitMs`.
- Bricks-pro standard: the paste's shape doesn't change; the drawer's own Offcanvas simply gets the site's real position at every width instead of a hole.
